01

Research

Seven interlocking areas. The through-line is a single question: how does health knowledge travel — from evidence, through education and technology, into everyday understanding and action?

  • 01

    Health Education

    Curriculum, pedagogy and learner-centred design for health competence.

  • 02

    Health Promotion

    Settings-based approaches, participation and community agency.

  • 03

    Digital Health

    Digital interventions, eHealth literacy and human-centred design.

  • 04

    Health Literacy

    Measurement, development and the equity dimensions of literacy.

  • 05

    Knowledge Translation

    Moving evidence into practice, policy and public understanding.

  • 06

    Educational Technology

    Learning design, simulation, games and interactive media.

  • 07

    AI & Health Education

    Responsible use of artificial intelligence in learning and research workflows.

05

Knowledge translation

Research is not finished when it is published. This is the part of the work that carries evidence outward — into language, formats and spaces where it can be understood and acted on.

  1. 01

    Research

    Questions asked rigorously, methods stated openly.

  2. 02

    Evidence

    Findings synthesised, weighed and contextualised.

  3. 03

    Translation

    Rewritten for the people who need it, not the people who produced it.

  4. 04

    Public understanding

    Clear language, honest uncertainty, accessible formats.

  5. 05

    Action

    Decisions, behaviours and policy that evidence can inform.
